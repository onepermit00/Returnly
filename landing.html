Good to know.

Will Back research every retailer automatically?

This first version includes a researched Fashion Nova reference. For other retailers, add the official policy link and confirm the terms yourself. Live AI policy research needs a connected service.

Can I get reminders when Back is closed?

Export daily reminders to your calendar and enable its alerts. Browser notifications work while Back is open. Background push is not connected in this version.

What if I bought something in a store?

Add the purchase date, select “In store,” and record the store’s return requirements. Your calendar and checklist work for store purchases too.

Where are my purchases saved?

On this browser, with no account needed. Export a backup from your space. Clearing browser data removes your local purchases; purchases don’t sync across devices.

Does the countdown guarantee a return?

No. Eligibility, exclusions, condition, shipping time, and exact cutoff times depend on the retailer. Confirm the official policy and your receipt before relying on a date.
